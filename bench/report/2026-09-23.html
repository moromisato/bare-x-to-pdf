How the score works

Each page from both engines is rasterised at 72 dpi. Ink overlap (IoU) takes the dark pixels of the reference page and of ours and divides what they share by everything either of them painted. It is a harsh, layout-first score: text stems are about one pixel wide at this size, so a line shifted by half a point loses most of its overlap even though a reader would never notice. As a rule of thumb, 85% and above is indistinguishable at reading size, 60 to 85% is the same layout with glyphs a fraction of a point off, and below 60% something sits in a different place.

Text extraction

A PDF that looks right but yields garbage when copied or indexed is not good enough. Both engines' PDFs go through the same extractor, bare-pdfium, and the words that come out are checked against the words in the source document itself, read from its XML. lo-footnote.docx21 of 21 of 2The footnote number sticks to the word before it in both PDFs, so 'Foo' comes out as 'Foo1'.
lo-footer-body-distance.docx1612 of 1612 of 16The two pages use the first-page and even-page headers and footers, so the four odd-page words never appear. The rest come out in page order (header, body, footer), which differs from the order in the file.

lo-2col-header.docx33 of 33 of 3All three words are there. The file lists body, footer, header; the page reads header, body, footer.

lo-ShapePlusImage.pptx88 of 80 of 8LibreOffice draws the WordArt as curves with no text layer, so its PDF yields no words at all.
lo-formatting-bullet-indent.pptx1313 of 1310 of 13bare-collabora spaces the title's letters apart ('Mast er st yl e'), so those three words do not match.

A word counts as found when it appears in the PDF text exactly, after Unicode normalisation, case-folding and unifying hyphen variants. A label or number glued to a word, or a value printed in a different form, therefore counts as missing for both engines alike.

Method

  • Corpus: 54 documents in bench/corpus/: real samples, a chart workbook, plus feature fixtures from LibreOffice's test suites (numbering, headers and footers, anchored pictures, footnotes, section breaks, borders, frames, columns, number formats).
  • Reference: bare-collabora 0.1.2 (darwin-arm64) converting the same files in the same process as the benchmark harness, bare bench/run.js.
  • Rasterisation: bare-pdfium at 72 dpi for both PDFs; ink overlap and pixel similarity in bench/lib/compare.js.
  • Text: bare bench/text.js extracts both PDFs with bare-pdfium and reads the source words from the document XML with a small zip reader in bench/lib/source-text.js; metrics in bench/lib/text-metrics.js.
  • Timing: bare scripts/timing.js ours|collabora <files> in separate processes; peak memory from /usr/bin/time -l.
  • Sizes: the darwin-arm64 prebuild directories on disk.
  • This page: python3 bench/report/generate.py over bench/out.
